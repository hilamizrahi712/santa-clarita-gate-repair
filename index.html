08 / FAQ

Common questions.

How fast can you respond to a gate repair call in Santa Clarita?

Same day in most cases. Our service vans run the SCV daily, so a technician is usually on-site within 2 hours of your call — often faster. For overnight emergencies, we dispatch 24/7.

Do you repair all brands of automatic gate openers?

Yes. We service LiftMaster, Eagle Access Control, All-O-Matic, Elite, Ramset, DoorKing, Linear, Viking, Chamberlain, Genie, and most other residential and commercial operators. Our vans carry common boards, motors, and sensors so most repairs close in one visit.

What does gate repair cost?

It depends on the fault. A safety sensor swap or limit adjustment typically runs under $200. A control board replacement is $350–$600. Full motor replacement ranges $500–$1,200 depending on brand and class. We quote on-site after diagnosis — no guessing over the phone.

Do you handle HOA gate projects in Valencia and Saugus?

Yes. We provide the elevation drawings, material specs, and color documentation that HOA design review requires. We've worked with communities throughout Valencia, Saugus, and Canyon Country and know the common approval workflows.
