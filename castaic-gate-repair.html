Can you build a gate wide enough for trucks and trailers in Castaic?

Yes. Castaic's ranch and Hasley Canyon properties need wide openings on heavy posts that clear a truck, trailer, or RV. We build extra-wide swing and sliding gates set back from the road so you're not stopping in traffic to wait.

Do you install fire-department access on Castaic gates?

We do. Much of Castaic sits in a high fire-hazard zone, so an automatic gate needs first-responder access — a Knox box, siren-activated entry, and a manual fail-safe. We install and wire these to local code so the fire department can always get in.

My Castaic driveway is long and off-grid-ish — can the gate be automated?

Absolutely. Long rural driveways off Hasley Canyon and Lake Hughes Road just need the right operator with solar or trenched power and a long-range keypad at the road. We size the system to the distance so it works in heat, wind, and dust.

How fast can you reach Castaic?

Usually same-day. We cover all of Castaic — Hillcrest, Hasley Canyon, and the lake area — and a technician is often at your gate within a couple of hours of your call.
