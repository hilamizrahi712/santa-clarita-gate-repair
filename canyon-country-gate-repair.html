Canyon Country gate FAQ

What Canyon Country
asks us.

Can you build an equestrian gate for a Sand Canyon property?

Yes. Sand Canyon's horse properties need wide, trailer-clearing openings and gates that look the part of an estate entrance. We build extra-wide swing and sliding gates on heavy posts, often in custom wrought iron, set back so you can pull off the road safely.

My Canyon Country driveway is long and dusty — will the automation hold up?

It will when it's built for it. Off Sand Canyon and Sierra Highway, driveways run long and dusty. We fit dust-sealed operators, solar or trenched power, and long-range keypads at the road so the gate is reliable in heat, wind, and grit.

Is fire access important for Canyon Country gates?

Very. Canyon Country backs onto brush and high fire-hazard terrain, so an automatic gate should have a Knox box, siren entry, and a manual fail-safe for first responders. We install these to local code.

How fast can you reach Canyon Country?

Usually same-day. We cover all of Canyon Country — Sand Canyon, Mint Canyon, and the Sierra Highway corridor — so a technician is often at your gate within a couple of hours of your call.
